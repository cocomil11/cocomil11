<h2 id="research">What we work on <span class="ps-jp">研究テーマ</span></h2>

My research sits at the intersection of **data visualization**, **virtual and augmented reality (VR/AR)**, and **human-AI interaction**. Broadly, I am interested in how new interfaces can make data easier to explore, to communicate, and to reason about — and in what happens to people when those interfaces are shared with an AI. Our work is published at venues such as CHI, UIST, VIS, ACM MM, and AAAI.

私の研究は、**データ可視化**・**VR/AR**・**人とAIのインタラクション**が交わる領域にあります。データを「探索する・伝える・考える」ための新しいインタフェースをデザインし、それを人がどう使うのかを実証的に評価する、というのが基本的な進め方です。

<div class="ps-cards">
  <div class="ps-card">
    <div class="ps-card-icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-chart-column"></i></div>
    <h3>Data Visualization<span class="ps-jp">データ可視化</span></h3>
    <p>Visualization design and perception, chart literacy, data storytelling, and tools that help non-experts read and author visualizations.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">可視化のデザインと知覚、データストーリーテリング、専門家でない人のための可視化ツール。</span></p>
  </div>
  <div class="ps-card">
    <div class="ps-card-icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-vr-cardboard"></i></div>
    <h3>Immersive Analytics (VR/AR)<span class="ps-jp">没入型可視化</span></h3>
    <p>Situated and embodied data representations, spatial interaction for analysis, and how immersion changes what people notice in data.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">空間に埋め込まれたデータ表現、身体を使った分析インタラクション。</span></p>
  </div>
  <div class="ps-card">
    <div class="ps-card-icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-comments"></i></div>
    <h3>Human-AI Interaction<span class="ps-jp">人とAIの対話</span></h3>
    <p>Interfaces where people and LLM-based agents analyze, design, or write together — including how much people should trust what the system shows them.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">人とAIエージェントが一緒に分析・デザインするインタフェース、AIへの信頼の設計。</span></p>
  </div>
  <div class="ps-card">
    <div class="ps-card-icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-hand-holding-heart"></i></div>
    <h3>Inclusive &amp; Social Applications<span class="ps-jp">社会実装</span></h3>
    <p>Applying the above to accessibility, education, healthcare, and ICT for humanitarian work, in collaboration with real stakeholders.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">アクセシビリティ、教育、医療、ICT4D など、実際の現場と組んだ研究。</span></p>
  </div>
</div>

You do not have to pick a topic from this list before contacting me. If you have your own idea and it overlaps with any of these directions, I would like to hear it — the best projects usually start from something the student actually cares about.

上記のリストから選ぶ必要はありません。ご自身の関心と重なる部分があれば、まずはその関心を聞かせてください。

For a concrete sense of the work, please look at my [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) and [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) pages, and read one or two papers that interest you before we talk.

<h2 id="who">Who I am looking for <span class="ps-jp">求める人物像</span></h2>

I care much more about attitude and curiosity than about your current skill set. Concretely, students tend to do well in my group if they:

私が重視するのは、現時点のスキルよりも、姿勢と好奇心です。次のような方と一緒に研究したいと考えています。

- **Are curious about people, not only about technology.** Our research is evaluated with real users, so an interest in why people behave the way they do matters as much as engineering skill.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">技術だけでなく「人」に関心があること。私たちの研究は実際のユーザで評価します。</span>
- **Can write and read.** Research is largely a writing job. You should be willing to read papers carefully and to rewrite your own text many times.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">読むこと・書くことを厭わないこと。研究の多くは執筆作業です。</span>
- **Can make something.** Programming (Python, JavaScript/D3, Unity, or similar) is our main way of asking questions. You need not be strong on day one, but you should enjoy building.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">手を動かしてつくれること。Python / JavaScript (D3) / Unity などを使います。最初から得意である必要はありません。</span>
- **Are comfortable with English, or willing to become so.** Our target venues and reviews are in English. Lab discussion can be in Japanese or English.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">英語に前向きであること。研究室での議論は日本語でも構いませんが、論文と査読は英語です。</span>
- **Can keep going when a study fails.** Most first results are negative. That is normal and not a reason to stop.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">うまくいかない時期を乗り越えられること。最初の結果はたいてい思い通りになりません。</span>

Backgrounds in computer science, design, psychology, statistics, or the social sciences are all welcome. I came to research after several years as a data scientist in industry, so I also enjoy working with students who want to keep one foot in practice.

情報学・デザイン・心理学・統計学・社会科学など、バックグラウンドは問いません。私自身が実務（データサイエンティスト）を経て研究の道に入ったため、実務と研究を往復したい方も歓迎します。

<h2 id="advising">What you can expect from me <span class="ps-jp">指導の方針</span></h2>

<div class="ps-cards ps-cards-2">
  <div class="ps-card">
    <h3>Regular one-on-one meetings<span class="ps-jp">個別ミーティング</span></h3>
    <p>Roughly weekly, plus a group meeting where we read papers and give feedback on each other's ongoing work. Meetings are for thinking together, not for reporting progress.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">週1回程度の個別ミーティングと、輪読・進捗共有のためのグループミーティングを行います。</span></p>
  </div>
  <div class="ps-card">
    <h3>Hands-on early, independent later<span class="ps-jp">段階的な自立</span></h3>
    <p>At the beginning I will be closely involved in scoping, study design, and writing. As you grow, I step back and the project becomes yours.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">最初は研究設計や執筆に密に関わり、慣れてきたら徐々に主導権をお渡しします。</span></p>
  </div>
  <div class="ps-card">
    <h3>Aiming at real venues<span class="ps-jp">対外発表</span></h3>
    <p>I will support you in submitting to international conferences, and in presenting and networking there. Publication is a goal, but not the only measure of a good thesis.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">国際会議への投稿・発表・海外の研究者との交流を積極的に支援します。</span></p>
  </div>
  <div class="ps-card">
    <h3>Careers beyond academia<span class="ps-jp">進路について</span></h3>
    <p>Many students go to industry, and that is a perfectly good outcome. I am happy to advise on internships, portfolios, and the transition between research and practice.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">産業界に進む方も歓迎です。インターンやポートフォリオの相談にも乗ります。</span></p>
  </div>
</div>

What I expect in return is straightforward: come to meetings prepared, tell me early when things are not working, and treat your labmates' time as seriously as your own.

こちらからお願いしたいことも単純です。ミーティングには準備して臨むこと、うまくいっていないときほど早めに共有すること、そして研究室のメンバーの時間を尊重することです。

<h2 id="contact">How to contact me <span class="ps-jp">連絡方法</span></h2>

Please email me at **ktakahira \[at\] connect.ust.hk**. I read every message from a prospective student, but I receive many generic ones, so a specific email is far more likely to get a substantive reply. A useful message includes:

メールは **ktakahira \[at\] connect.ust.hk** までお送りください。いただいたメールにはすべて目を通していますが、定型文のような内容には返信が難しいことがあります。以下を含めていただけると助かります。

<div class="ps-template" markdown="1">
**Subject / 件名:** `Prospective student — [Master's / PhD / undergrad / research student], [your name]`

1. **Who you are** — your current program, institution, and expected graduation.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">所属・学年・卒業予定時期</span>
2. **What you want to work on** — two or three sentences on the question that interests you, and why.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">取り組みたいテーマと、その理由（2〜3文で）</span>
3. **Why my group** — mention one paper or project of mine you have read and what you thought about it.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">私の論文・プロジェクトを1つ挙げ、それについての感想</span>
4. **What you have built** — a CV, transcript, GitHub, or portfolio, whichever fits you best.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">CV・成績・GitHub・ポートフォリオなど</span>
5. **Your timeline** — which intake you are aiming for, and your funding situation if relevant.<br><span class="ps-jp-body">希望する入学時期、必要であれば経済的な状況</span>
</div>

If you have not heard back within about two weeks, a polite reminder is welcome — it usually means the message was buried, not ignored.

2週間ほど返信がない場合は、遠慮なくリマインドしてください。見落としている可能性が高いです。

<h2 id="faq">FAQ <span class="ps-jp">よくある質問</span></h2>

<div class="ps-faq" markdown="1">

**Do I need a computer science background?**<br>
No. Students from design, psychology, statistics, and the social sciences do well here, as long as you are willing to learn to program. What you cannot skip is the willingness to build and to run studies with real people.<br>
<span class="ps-jp-body">情報系の出身である必要はありません。デザイン・心理・統計・社会科学の出身の方も歓迎します。ただし、プログラミングを学ぶ意欲は必要です。</span>

**Do I need Japanese?**<br>
Not for research. Coursework and administrative procedures may involve Japanese depending on the program, so please confirm the requirements on the official admissions pages. Learning some Japanese will make life in Kyoto much better regardless.<br>
<span class="ps-jp-body">研究上は日本語は必須ではありません。ただし履修や事務手続きで日本語が必要な場合があるため、公式ページをご確認ください。</span>

**Can you guarantee admission or funding?**<br>
No. Admission is decided by the graduate school's examination, and funding depends on scholarships and project grants that are outside my sole control. I can tell you honestly whether I would like to supervise you, and I can advise on which funding routes to pursue.<br>
<span class="ps-jp-body">合格や経済的支援を個人として保証することはできません。ただし、指導を希望するかどうかは率直にお伝えし、支援制度の探し方は一緒に考えます。</span>

**How many students do you take?**<br>
A small number each year, so that everyone gets real supervision time. If I am full, I will say so early rather than leave you waiting.<br>
<span class="ps-jp-body">一人ひとりに十分な時間を割くため、受け入れ人数は年に数名程度です。難しい場合は早めにお伝えします。</span>

**Can I visit the lab before applying?**<br>
Yes, and I recommend it. Talking to current students tells you more about a lab than any web page — including this one.<br>
<span class="ps-jp-body">見学は歓迎ですし、おすすめします。実際に在籍している学生と話すのが、研究室を知る一番の方法です。</span>

**I am not a student, but I would like to collaborate or invite you to speak.**<br>
Please email me with the same level of detail. I take on writing, training, and consulting work related to data visualization when the schedule allows.<br>
<span class="ps-jp-body">学生以外の方からの共同研究・執筆・研修のご依頼も承っています。同様にメールでご連絡ください。</span>

</div>

<div class="ps-closing" markdown="1">
Choosing an advisor is a bigger decision than choosing a topic; a topic can change, an advisor usually does not. I would rather have an honest conversation early than have either of us discover a mismatch two years in. Please write.

研究テーマは変えられますが、指導教員はそう簡単には変えられません。だからこそ、早い段階で率直に話しておきたいと考えています。まずはお気軽にご連絡ください。
</div>
